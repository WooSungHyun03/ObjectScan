## Type and rhythm

Self-hosted @fontsource-variable/noto-sans-kr. Korean-first, word-break:keep-all with overflow-wrap:anywhere for long content. Headings60/44/38/30/24; body16/15/14; utility12/11 only. 1.65 body leading, restrained negative heading tracking. Desktop page max1440, margins20–72. Spacing4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64; control radius10, panels20, grouped inset controls6. No decorative gradients/glow/glass or repeated feature-card trios.

## States and motion

Buttons44px minimum, controls use control-border, primary text on-accent; focus2px focus token with4px offset. Press feedback1px, transitions180–200ms only for state/feedback. Reduced motion removes animation and smooth scrolling. Never show invented success, financial amounts or unavailable generation. Errors remain actionable Korean and files private.

## Page density

Landing: airy product preview, one primary creation intent, no WebGL on initial load. Create: progressive mode/file selection, memory-only draft handoff with explicit upload. Workspace: denser task surfaces and real queue states. Editor: dedicated canvas/tools, numeric alternatives, mobile bottom sheet. Marketplace: real listings only plus a clearly labeled non-sale public example. See pages/ overrides.
